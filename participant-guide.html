Ideal Technical Setup

For the best experience and recording quality, here are our recommended specifications. Don't worry if you don't have the ideal setup - we can work with what you have!

Internet Connection

Minimum Speed 10 Mbps up/down
Recommended 25+ Mbps up/down
Connection Type Wired (Ethernet) preferred

Test Your Speed: Visit speedtest.net to check your connection. Look at both download AND upload speeds.

Video (Camera)

Minimum Resolution 720p HD
Recommended 1080p Full HD
Frame Rate 30 fps

Most laptops built after 2018 have HD webcams. External USB webcams like Logitech C920/C922 work great.

Audio (Microphone)

Best: USB microphone or headset with mic
Good: Wired earbuds with microphone
Acceptable: Built-in laptop/phone microphone

Important: Use headphones or earbuds to prevent echo. Low-profile earbuds look best on camera!

Computer/Device

Best: Desktop or laptop computer
Good: Tablet (iPad, Android tablet)
Works: Smartphone (stable mount recommended)

Browser: Chrome or Edge work best. Make sure your browser is up to date.

Before the Call

1

Close Other Applications

Shut down Zoom, Skype, games, streaming services, and any other apps that might use your camera, microphone, or internet bandwidth.

2

Find a Quiet Space

Choose a room where you won't be interrupted. Let others in your home know you'll be recording. Turn off TVs, radios, and silence your phone.

3

Set Up Good Lighting

Face a window or lamp so light shines on your face (not behind you). Avoid sitting with a bright window behind you - it will make you appear dark.

4

Check Your Background

A clean, uncluttered background works best. A plain wall, bookshelf, or tidy room looks professional. Remove anything you don't want on camera.

5

Plug In Your Device

Make sure your laptop or phone is plugged into power. Video calls drain batteries quickly, and you don't want to run out mid-recording.

Joining the Call

Step 1: Get Your Link

Our director will send you a special link via email or text message. This is your personal invitation to join the podcast room.

Step 2: Click and Join

When you click the link, it will open in your web browser. Look for the button that says "Join Room with Webcam" and click it.

Step 3: Allow Camera & Microphone

Your browser will ask permission to use your camera and microphone. Click "Allow" or "Yes" when prompted.

If you accidentally clicked "Block", you'll need to reset permissions in your browser settings.

Step 4: Select Your Devices

If you have multiple cameras or microphones, you can choose which ones to use from the dropdown menus.

  • Camera: Select one and check if you can see yourself on screen
  • Microphone: Speak or clap - if the audio meter moves, you're good!

Step 5: You're In!

Once connected, you'll see yourself on screen and the director will guide you from there. Relax and be yourself!

Troubleshooting

Camera or microphone not working?

1. Make sure no other apps are using your camera/mic (close Zoom, Skype, etc.)

2. Check that you clicked "Allow" when the browser asked for permission

3. Try refreshing the page

4. If using Chrome, click the camera icon in the address bar to check permissions

Video is choppy or freezing?

1. Close other browser tabs and applications

2. If on WiFi, try moving closer to your router

3. If possible, use a wired ethernet connection

4. Ask others in your home to pause streaming/downloads temporarily

Can't hear anyone?

1. Check that your computer/device volume is turned up

2. Make sure headphones are plugged in correctly

3. Check the audio output selection in the VDO.Ninja settings

4. Try using the device's built-in speakers instead of headphones

Others can't hear me?

1. Make sure you're not muted (check for mute button in VDO.Ninja)

2. Verify the correct microphone is selected

3. Speak louder or move closer to your microphone

4. Check if the audio meter moves when you speak

Echo or feedback during the call?

1. Use headphones! This is the #1 fix for echo

2. Lower your speaker volume

3. Move your microphone away from your speakers

4. Use earbuds with a built-in microphone
