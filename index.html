In Loving Memory

An Original Founder

MB

Mike Baker

Original Founder · Veteran, U.S. Army

With heavy hearts, the Veteran’s Scene family says farewell to Mike Baker, one of our original founders and a proud soldier of the United States Army who answered his nation’s call during the Vietnam War. Mike helped lay the foundation for everything this community stands for — giving veterans a voice, a place to be heard, and a family that never forgets its own.

We salute you, Mike. Thank you for your service to our great nation, for your courage, and for the legacy you leave behind in every story we share. Your watch is over, and you have earned your rest. You will not be forgotten.

“All gave some. Some gave all. All are remembered.”

Our deepest condolences and gratitude go out to Mike’s family. May you find comfort in knowing his legacy lives on. Rest easy, soldier — we have the watch from here.
