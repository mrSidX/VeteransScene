Easy Podcast Join Guide

VDO.Ninja is a free, simple tool that lets you join our video podcast using your phone or computer’s web browser. No apps to download, no account needed—just a link! Follow these steps to get set up.

Before You Start

Close any apps or programs running on your phone or computer (like Zoom, Skype, or games) to avoid slowdowns or camera/microphone issues.

Step 1: Get Your Link

Our director will send you a special link to join the podcast. You’ll get it by:

  • Email: If using a computer, check your email and click the link.
  • Text Message: If using a phone, check your texts and tap the link.

When the link opens in your browser, click “Join Room with Webcam” to start.

Step 2: Say “Yes” to Camera & Mic

Your browser will ask if it’s okay to use your camera and microphone. Click “Allow” or “Yes” to let us see and hear you.

Step 3: Pick Your Camera & Mic

If you have more than one camera or microphone, you’ll see a dropdown menu to choose them.

  • Camera: Pick a camera. If you see yourself on the screen, it’s the right one!
  • Microphone: Pick a mic. Speak or clap—if the green audio bars bounce, we can hear you!
More: How to Choose Camera & Mic

On your screen, look for a small camera or gear icon (usually at the top or bottom). Click it to see a list of your cameras and mics. Try each one until you see yourself and the audio bars move when you talk.

Step 4: You’re Ready!

If you see yourself and the audio bars move when you talk, you’re good to go! You’ll be in the podcast session with us. The director will guide you from there.

Tips for a Great Podcast

  • Use Headphones or Earbuds: This stops echo so we hear you clearly. Low-profile earbuds work great!
  • Good Microphone: Use a headset mic or your device’s built-in mic. Avoid cheap or distant mics for better sound.
  • Well-Lit Room: Sit in a bright room or near a lamp so we can see you clearly.
  • Strong Internet: Use Wi-Fi or a wired connection on a computer for the best quality. Test your speed at Speedtest.net. Aim for at least 10 Mbps download/upload for smooth video.
  • If Using a Phone: Make sure you have a strong signal (4G/5G or Wi-Fi). Avoid areas with bad reception to prevent dropouts.
  • Best Setup: Use a computer or laptop at home with a wired internet connection (Ethernet) or strong Wi-Fi for the smoothest experience.
