Join Our Program

Veteran's Scene is a platform dedicated to amplifying veteran voices. We believe every service member has a unique story worth sharing - whether it's about deployment experiences, transition challenges, lessons learned in uniform, or the path to finding purpose after service.

What We Offer

  • One-on-one interviews - Share your story in a focused, respectful conversation
  • Panel discussions - Join other veterans in roundtable conversations on important topics
  • Flexible recording options - Remote via Zoom/VDO.Ninja or in-person when possible
  • Technical support - We'll guide you through setup and ensure quality recording
  • Your comfort matters - Control what you share and how your story is told
Apply to Join Us

Who Should Apply

All veterans are welcome. Whether you:

  • • Served in combat or support roles
  • • Deployed once or multiple times
  • • Completed your contract or made a career
  • • Are recently separated or decades removed
  • • Want to discuss challenges or triumphs
  • • Have advice for transitioning service members

The Application Process

Fill out our simple application form sharing a bit about your service and what you'd like to discuss. Our team will review your submission and reach out to schedule a recording that works with your schedule. No pressure, no scripts - just authentic conversation.
