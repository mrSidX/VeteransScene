Thank You for Your Interest!

Your application has been received. We're honored that you're considering sharing your story with Veteran's Scene.

What Happens Next?

1

Application Review

Our team will review your application within a few days. We read every submission carefully.

2

We'll Reach Out

If we'd like to move forward, a team member will contact you using your preferred method. No pressure - we respect your time.

3

Pre-Recording Briefing

If selected, you'll receive a briefing call before any recording. We'll discuss topics, answer questions, and make sure you're comfortable.

4

Share Your Story

When you're ready, we'll schedule a time that works for you. Participation is always voluntary - you're in control.

Ways We Can Connect

We offer flexible options to make participation as comfortable as possible for you:

Video Podcast

Join us remotely via video chat from the comfort of your home. Uses simple browser-based technology - no apps to install.

Audio Call

Prefer voice only? We can arrange a phone or audio-only session. Your story matters more than being on camera.

In-Person

Local to the area? Join us in our studio for a face-to-face conversation in a comfortable setting.

If You're Selected

You'll Be Briefed

  • ✓ Technical setup assistance if joining remotely

  • ✓ You choose what to share and what to keep private
  • ✓ Anonymous participation is respected if requested
  • ✓ Review before publication (when applicable)
